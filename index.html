Snowpack Explorer

What this shows

Each day's snowpack, ranked against the same date in past years.

  • D0–D4, yellow to red: less snow than usual.
  • Near normal, white: 30th to 70th percentile.
  • W0–W4, light to dark blue: more snow than usual.
Which normals should I use?

Each choice turns past years into a normal range for the date.

  • Smoothed is the default. It fits one curve through the whole snow year. It changes least from day to day.
  • Fitted fits a curve to each calendar day alone.
  • Empirical ranks this year directly against past years. It is the noisiest. It cannot rank beyond the record.

The three differ most early and late in the season and at low elevations.

How to use it

  • Search a place, watershed, ski area or ZIP code.
  • Step the Date with ‹ and ›.
  • Tap the map for its percentile, or a Zonal watershed for its plots.
  • Tap SNOTEL, then a station, for its plot.
  • Keys: / search, ← → change the day, Esc close. Add ?kbd=off to the address to turn off / and the arrows.
What the plots show

A plot covers the whole snow year. The colored bands are the range of past years. The black line is this year.

HypsomeSWE splits a watershed's snow into 100-foot elevation bands.

Plots are not published for every date. The panel then offers the nearest date that has them. Data for this plot lists the numbers.
